Multiaccount Publish

A private tool that prepares short videos and posts them to its owner's own TikTok account.

What it does

The owner supplies a source video they are entitled to use. The tool retrieves it, analyses it, selects a passage, renders a vertical subtitled clip of at most ninety seconds, and lets the owner review the result before anything leaves the machine.

Once a clip is approved, the tool sends it to the owner's own TikTok account through the official Content Posting API. Nothing is sent without an explicit order placed by the owner, and no publication is ever reported as successful without proof returned by the platform.

Who it is for

One person: the owner of the account it posts to. There is no sign-up, no second user, and no service offered to anyone else. Access is limited to a single administrator account.

What it does not do

  • It does not post on behalf of third parties, and it manages no account other than the owner's own.
  • It does not publish automatically on a schedule of its own: every send is ordered by the owner.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
