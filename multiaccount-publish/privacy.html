Privacy Policy

Multiaccount Publish. Last updated: 17 September 2026.

Who this concerns

One person: the owner of the installation and of the TikTok account it posts to. The tool has no other user, collects nothing from visitors, and is not open to the public.

What is stored

  • TikTok authorisation. A refresh token, its expiry, the account identifier returned by TikTok, and the list of permissions granted. These are held server side only and are never sent to the browser.
  • Publication records. The publication identifier returned by TikTok, the state of each send, and the platform's own error codes, so that a lost response can be reconciled rather than resent.
  • Video files and captions supplied by the owner, and the clips produced from them.

What is not stored

  • No password of any kind, for TikTok or otherwise.
  • No data about other TikTok users, no follower lists, no third-party profiles.
  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of visitors to this site.

Where it is stored

In the owner's own cloud account, in the Ireland region. Access is restricted to the owner's administrator account and to the server functions that perform the sending.

How it is used

Solely to send the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account, and to report accurately on what happened. Nothing is sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

Authorisation tokens are kept until they are revoked or expire. Publication records are kept as a history of what was sent. Video files are deleted when the owner purges the job they belong to.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect the application at any time from TikTok's own settings, under security and permissions. Doing so invalidates the stored token immediately, and the tool can no longer send anything. Deleting the installation removes all stored data with it.
